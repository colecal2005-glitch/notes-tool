## Anti-patterns — never do these

**Opening moves that kill engagement:**
- Starting with an emoji: "🚀 Here's why..." / "✨ Something I learned..." / "💡 Hot take:"
- Starting with hashtags: "#ContentCreators, here's the truth..."
- Starting with "I" — weak, self-absorbed opening
- Starting with "So," or "Look," — filler

**AI tells that destroy credibility:**
- "Delve into"
- "Navigate the landscape"
- "It's worth noting that"
- "Game-changer" / "paradigm shift" / "unlock your potential"
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "Let's unpack this"
- "At the end of the day"
- Ending with "What do you think?" as a lazy engagement prompt

**Structural failures:**
- Summarizing the post instead of provoking curiosity about it
- Lists longer than 5 items (use prose or cut items)
- "Thread:" followed by numbered points — this isn't Twitter
- Parenthetical asides in every sentence
- Bolding random phrases throughout the Note

**Tone failures:**
- Corporate hedging: "It may be the case that..." / "One could argue..."
- False balance: "On one hand... on the other hand..." (Notes demand a take)
- Humble-bragging setup: "I never thought I'd say this, but..."
- Vague inspiration: "Your story matters." / "Keep going." / "You've got this."

**Format failures:**
- Notes over 300 characters that don't earn the length
- Questions so open they require no thought to skip ("What's your favorite way to grow on Substack?")
- Links buried so deep readers don't see them
- "Read more here →" as the only reason to click

**AI language tells — words and constructions that read as machine-written:**
- Significance inflation: "stands as a testament to", "marks a pivotal moment", "underscores its importance", "represents a shift", "indelible mark", "deeply rooted"
- Promotional filler: "groundbreaking", "vibrant", "breathtaking", "nestled", "in the heart of", "renowned"
- Copula avoidance: "serves as", "stands as", "functions as" — just say "is"
- Tacked-on -ing phrases: "highlighting how...", "underscoring the importance of...", "showcasing..." — these add fake depth to the end of sentences
- Negative parallelism: "It's not just X, it's Y" / "Not merely X, but Y" — overused structure
- Persuasive framing: "The real question is", "At its core", "What really matters", "Fundamentally"
- Signposting: "Let's dive in", "Here's what you need to know", "Let's explore", "Without further ado"
- Rule of three forced on everything: three adjectives, three examples, three takeaways — vary the count
- Em dash overuse: one em dash per Note maximum; prefer a comma or a period
- Generic conclusions: "The future looks bright", "Exciting times ahead", "This is just the beginning"
- Vague authority: "Experts say", "Studies show", "Industry observers note" — name the source or cut it
